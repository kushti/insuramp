Four pillars of state independent 21st century money:

* programmable trust-minimized neutral ledger and reserve asset
* trust minimized derivatives, stablecoins and so on
* trust minimized crosschaining and onramp
* trust minimized money (as MoE) creation on top

Ergo ecosystem is have all four, for the first time in "crypto", with transparent trust minimization on all the levels. 

Note, the four pillars is just a framework, and it shows how can a trust minimized reserve asset and its ledger found a way to produce value for the real world. Surely, there is need for DeFi tooling (decentralized exchanges, onchain lending etc), privacy and so on, but such things are framing the pillars and build convenient on-chain trading for value brought from outside (worse, on value which is created on-chain and makes sense on chain only, such sandboxed games are just extracting liquidity brought on-chain when outside markets looks brighter than crypto space promises). 

Currently value mostly brought via centralized trusted services and stablecoins. Our vision is to democratize and p2p-ize money markets on all the levels, make a lot of new millionaires, make communities disconnected from fiat money printers thriving, and take a piece of pie from all the processes which already take place and will inevitably dominate in the 21st century.

Lets go step-by-step now:

* first layer: programmable trust-minimized neutral ledger and reserve asset - Ergo:

unlike Bitcoin, Ergo already has (Bitcoin has none). It is Proof-of-Work, so miners are making money, early holders and ones who are buying the bottoms also interested in its future. Ergo is now in tail emission mode, so big movements are possible. I think this layer should be developed as open-source software. There is good possibility at some point to form DAT (digital asset treasury) to lock ERG. There are interesting possibilities in combinining buying ERG and then minting stablecoins on the top. 

* second layer:  trust minimized derivatives, stablecoins and so on

USE stablecoin is providing superior 1:1 backing during mint, while being decentralized autonomous crypto-collateralized stablecoin. With its pegging rules it is attractive to arbitrage/second-market market-makers, they are already establishing pools around

* third layer: Rosen bridge and more upcoming tools:

Rosen is paranoid-security bridge connecting Ergo, Bitcoin, Cardano, Ethereum, BSC, Firo, soon Base and Nervos. Current market capitalization is $8.7M just, and tokens allocated for team/investors are not distributed yet. Bridge TVL is $1.2M , the main issue is that it connects Ergo and Cardano now mostly, but there is a plan how to bootstrap other directions. 

It looks quite promising to use Ergo as insurance tool for p2p onramping, such as fiat to USDT or fiat to BTC / Monero. With this,  Ergo can be used as collateral by a digital asset seller, to eliminate trust. Eliminating trust in seller would democratize p2p markets a lot, making Ergo a working horse even if in 99% cases it is not claimed by a buyer, and so grow Ergo TVL significantly.


* fourth layer: Basis for monetary expansion

Attempts to make P2P cash over the Internet started before Bitcoin, see, for example, "Peer-to-peer money: free currency over the internet" 
by Kenji Saito from 2003, or original RipplePay idea and service by Ryan Fugger from 2005. Cryptocurrency 
space ignored earlier work and started own attempts to do p2p cash, such as Lightning / Cashu / Fedimint etc.

Thus we have two non-intersecting worlds: original p2p cash which is based on p2p trust, and cryptocurrency-backed which 
does require for 100% backing with cryptocurrencies. We combine the best from two worlds in Basis: 
 * money issuance can be based purely on trust
 * optionally, on-chain reserves on Ergo can back issued p2p cash
 * it is up to a peer to demand for backing, to choose whom to trust, whom to blacklist etc
 * thus this is providing self-sovereign control on what kind of money (and so risk) to accept 
 * we also call it free digital banking on steroids

Basis is a low-level framework which can be used in many monetary applications, such as:
* community currencies (LETS, local currencies etc)
* value transfer networks, informal (such as Hawala) and formal 
* agentic economies

and so on

There could be multiple coexisting Basis based communities (using different instances of the same software). They can always have economic connections via on-chain reserves, it would be good to 
explore more efficient options.
